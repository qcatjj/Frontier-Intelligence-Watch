TRANSPARENCY STANDARD

What this tracker does—and doesn't—tell us.

01 / No fabricated percentages

There is no accepted scientific test that converts research progress into a percentage of superintelligence. A percentage on one specific benchmark is not a percentage of the way to ASI.

02 / Real measurements only

Every chart uses the published units of its source: capability-index points, model ratings, annual release counts or billions invested. Never combine them into a mystery "ASI score."

03 / Dates matter

Country investment figures are from 2025. Some ratings are from March 2026 and capability-index estimates are from October 2026. A dated historical comparison is not a live leaderboard.
